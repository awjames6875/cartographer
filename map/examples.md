# examples.md
**Three walks, start to finish**
Drawn: August 22, 2026 · Companion to `catalog.md`

These show how the folder is read. They are not instructions to copy. The account
keeps moving, and these walks carry dates like everything else here.

Every walk is the same shape: **`catalog.md` → one card → stop.**

---

## 1. "Change the confirmation message."

**Hop 1 — `catalog.md`.** That sentence is listed under **Lane C**, they booked.
Lane C answers it before you open anything:

> **"Change the confirmation message" is Lane G, not this lane.**

`SH - Fit Call Booked` sends nothing at all — no text, no email · settled: Sadie,
August 19, 2026. Open it looking for wording and you will find tags.

The catalog sent you one lane over. That is hop one doing its job.

**Hop 2 — `objects/safe-harbor-fit-call.md`.** Lane G's card. The confirmation
message lives on the calendar. The calendar auto-confirms and sends its own
message · settled: Sadie, August 19, 2026.

**Stop.** Open `Calendars → "Safe Harbor Fit Call"` and read the message there.

**What the walk saved you.** Lane C is the obvious guess, and it is empty. You
would have opened a workflow, found nothing but tags, and had no reason to think
the wording lived somewhere else.

## 2. "Update the Lead Flow Vo2 workflow."

**Hop 1 — `catalog.md`.** Type that name into the account and two come back.
Both are on this page:

- **`Safe Harbor Consultation Lead Flow Vo2`** — Lane A. LIVE.
- **`GOLDEN COPY - Lead Flow Vo2`** — under **Named, and nothing flowing
  through**. Reported a backup draft. Reported never published. No card.

**Stop here. There is no second hop.**

Nothing in **Named, and nothing flowing through** has a card, because nobody is
being sent to change those. They are written down so you recognise one when you
land on it, and know it is not what you came for.

Two things that entry tells you, and one thing it does not:

- It sorts under **G**, not **S**. A search box returns both. Scrolling down the
  list past the S names does not.
- It says **VERIFY — mark not confirmed.** Never published is what was
  *reported*. Nobody read the switch. The map does not know, and says so.

**This map has no ghosts today.** A ghost is a name somebody searched the account
for and did not find. Two names once sat there. Both turned out to be real, so
both moved.

## 3. "Change the message someone gets when they miss the call."

**Hop 1 — `catalog.md`.** Lane D. It names `SH - Fit Call No Show`.

**Hop 2 — `objects/sh-fit-call-no-show.md`.** The card names what else moves.
All settled: Sadie, August 19, 2026.

- It sends **one** rebooking text, carrying the booking link.
- Then it **waits 2 days** and checks whether the contact has re-booked.
- If they have not, it adds the tag `fit call nurture` — **which starts
  `SH - Fit Call Nurture (Alt Days)` from its first message.** That is 15 texts,
  2 days apart, about four weeks end to end.

So the message you were sent to change is one text. The wait sitting underneath
it is a door into a four-week sequence.

**And none of it runs until a person clicks.** The platform does not mark an
appointment No Show when the time passes. Somebody opens the appointment and
changes the status by hand · settled: Sadie, August 19, 2026. If nobody does
that, this workflow never fires.

**Stop.** Open `Automation → Workflows → "SH - Fit Call No Show"`.
