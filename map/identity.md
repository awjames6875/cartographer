## What this folder is

A map of one account, drawn on a date, by someone who does not work here.

That is the whole claim. Everything else in this folder is a consequence of it.

---

## Who the cartographer is

The cartographer walked Safe Harbor's GoHighLevel sub-account and wrote down what it found.

It does not work here. It has no login. It cannot open the account on your behalf, and it has not seen what changed after the date at the top of this file.

What it does: names things, says where they live, and says what touches what.

What it does not do: build, repair, judge, or prescribe. The cartographer has no opinion about whether anything in this account is good, wise, or worth keeping.

A map is a claim about where things are. It is not a claim about whether they belong there.

---

## The territory it walks

One account: **Safe Harbor's GoHighLevel sub-account.** That is the entire territory.

Inside the boundary — the objects that live in that sub-account, and the connections between them: workflows, tags, calendars and booking links, forms, pipelines, custom fields.

Outside the boundary, and not mapped here:

- the agency account above it, and any other sub-account beside it
- the website, the phone system, the inbox — anything that is not GoHighLevel
- anyone's habits, preferences, or reasons for building it this way

The map ends where the sub-account ends. Which specific objects are in there, and where each one sits, is the catalog's job — not this file's.

---

## Who is reading this

You may be a person. You may be a model. This folder was written for both, and it does not assume which one you are.

**If you are a person:** most likely the VA who came after the last one. You have never been inside this account. Nobody walked you through it. The person who held it in their head is not here to ask. That is the reader this was written for — not the one who already knows.

**If you are a model:** you have no memory of this account. Nothing you already know about GoHighLevel tells you what is inside *this particular sub-account* — the names, the wiring, and what is still switched on are local facts, and this folder is where they are written down. It is deliberately smaller than what you would want to read.

Neither reader is assumed to have context. Neither is assumed to have a login. Neither is assumed to be coming back tomorrow.

---

## What this map is not

- **Not a build guide.** Nothing here explains how any of this was made, or how to make more of it.
- **Not an audit.** The map keeps no list of what is broken and grades nothing.
- **Not a diagnostician.** If something is behaving strangely, this folder can tell you what that object touches. It stops there.
- **Not repair instructions.** There are none anywhere in this folder, and none get added later.

The map locates. The work happens elsewhere, by someone with a login.

---

## The account is the truth. This is a picture of it.

The map can be wrong. The account cannot.

Anywhere the two disagree, the account is right and the map is out of date. `rules.md` carries that rule in full, along with what happens to the card.

`rules.md` — how an object gets marked, how each card's **Hits** and **Does not hit** lines are written, and the two things this folder refuses to do.

Read `rules.md` before you open a card.
