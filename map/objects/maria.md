**Maria is outside the boundary**

`identity.md` draws the territory as one thing: Safe Harbor's GoHighLevel sub-account. It puts the phone system, and anything else that is not GoHighLevel, outside that line.

Maria runs on VAPI. VAPI is not GoHighLevel. She is outside.

What this card maps is the part of her that is inside — her **footprint**: whichever objects in this sub-account start her, receive from her, or get written to by her. Those are GoHighLevel objects, they sit in the territory, and the ordinary rules apply to them.

What this card does not map, and what does not get added later: what she says on a call, how she is configured, what her script is, or anything else on the VAPI side. Those things exist. They are somewhere. That somewhere is not in this folder and not in this account, and the map stops at the line rather than reaching across it.

---

**The mark is declined, not deferred**

This is the one card in the folder where those are different things.

On every other card the mark is unearned because nobody has walked the account yet, and a walk settles it.

Here a walk does not settle it. **LIVE** requires that the object was found *in the account* (`rules.md` §1). No walk of this sub-account will find Maria, however carefully it is done, because she is not in the territory. A mark she cannot earn is not a mark that is pending. It is a mark this map has no standing to give her.

**Her footprint can be marked.** Whichever GHL objects turn out to start her or receive from her are ordinary objects on the ordinary rules, and each can be LIVE or LEFTOVER on its own card. None of them are identified yet, so none of them have cards yet.
